About Us

Group 14-1B-BSIT

We created a website about three popular cat breeds: Persian, Maine Coon, and Siamese. Through this project, we improved our knowledge of each breed, including their appearance, personality, behavior, and care needs. At the same time, we developed our skills in HTML and CSS, while learning how to organize information, design web pages, and present content in a clear and attractive way. This project helped us combine our interest in cats with practical web design and content presentation skills.

The purpose of this website is to provide simple and useful information about Persian, Maine Coon, and Siamese cats while helping us improve our web design, HTML, CSS, and content presentation skills.
